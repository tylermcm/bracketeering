# bracketeer 0.2.0 — grouping review, "China 8-26"

Ground-truth review of a real shoot, done by a Claude Code session on the photographer's machine with
exiftool and preview inspection. It drove the fixes in 0.3.0. Frame names refer to the photographer's
files, which are not in this repository.

Reviewed 2026-11-02. Camera: one NIKON Z 7_2 (serial 3041025), 1131 NEF + JPEG pairs, 6 JPEG-only frames
(`DSC_1417`–`DSC_1422`), and a `_winners/` subfolder holding 18 copied picks. No photos were modified or moved.

## How this was checked

- Runs: `bracketeer scan . --pixels` (native backend and `--backend exiftool`, exiftool 12.40), plus a
  `--no-recursive` control run.
- **Ground truth for brackets:** Nikon maker notes. `ShootingMode` contains "Exposure Bracketing" whenever AEB
  is on, and `ExposureBracketValue` gives each frame's slot in the program (`BracketProgram` = 5F:
  0, −4/3, −2/3, +2/3, +4/3, or 3F). Camera bracket cycles were rebuilt from those tags in `ShutterCount`
  order (continuous across the `_DSC`/`DSC_` filename prefix switch).
- **Ground truth for framing:** embedded previews (contact sheets), backed by phase-correlation shift between
  previews (peak > ~0.15 registers cleanly; ~0.02–0.05 = recomposed).
- **Grouping rule:** frames from one camera bracket cycle form one bracket, *unless* the shooter recomposed
  partway. In that case the cycle splits into one bracket per composition, and a lone frame becomes a single.

## 2. Bursts 1746–1750 / 1751–1755

5.6 fps each; ShutterCount continuous (20525–20534); 16.71 s gap; AF re-acquired at 1751 (focus distance
199.5 m → 50 m); reframed. Two presses — both backends correct. The Z 7_2 writes no burst sequence number
(`SequenceNumber` empty, `ShootingMode = Continuous`).

## 3. `_winners/` subfolder

The scan recursed into `_winners/`; each copied pick was grouped with its own original as a 2-frame
"burst at 500 fps" (18 groups). Two originals were pulled out of real bursts (`_DSC0824` with `_DSC0825`,
`_DSC1158` with `_DSC1157`). Fix: dedupe by (body serial, ShutterCount) or exclude the folder.

## 4. Near-duplicate scenes

15 sampled scenes: all coherent. But same-subject shots were split into different scenes in at least
11 places, e.g. `_DSC0878`–`_DSC0894` (one ivy-covered-overpass composition, 17 frames in 30 s, 5–8 % shifts)
cut into 11 scenes; `_DSC0734`–`_DSC0761` into 3; `_DSC1066`→`1067`; `DSC_1232`–`1236`; `_DSC1706`→`1707`;
`_DSC1724`→`1725`. Cause: 9×8 dHash compared only with the previous group's representative, threshold 12
bits. On fine textures a 5–8 % handheld drift flips 13–29 bits (0879↔0880 = 19, 0880↔0881 = 29,
1706↔1707 = 17, 1066↔1067 = 13).

## 5. exiftool backend bug

`classify_bracket()` returned `('exposure', None)` for every Nikon frame, including ones with bracketing off,
because Nikon writes configuration tags on every frame (`BracketSet: AE`, `AutoBracketModeM: Flash/Speed`)
and `BracketProgram: Disabled` wasn't recognised as off. Reliable Nikon signals: `ShootingMode` containing
"Exposure Bracketing", `BracketProgram` ≠ "Disabled", `ExposureBracketValue` per frame.

## 6. Other observations

- Text output prints basenames only, which hid the `_winners/` duplicates.
- Group times are UTC; the camera writes local time with `-07:00`.
- 6 JPEG-only frames handled as singles; no unreadable files.
